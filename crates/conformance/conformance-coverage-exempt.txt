# Format: `<crate> <requirement-id>: <reason>`. Requirement ids are the `id`s of
# faucet_conformance::required::REQUIREMENTS; `scripts/conformance-coverage.py`

# requirement reads crates/conformance/call-deadline-exempt.txt instead.
